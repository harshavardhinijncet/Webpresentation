Brand logos for the Centers of Excellence page.

One file per logo, named after the brand in lowercase with hyphens:
  microsoft.svg   aws.svg   red-hat.png   uipath.svg

SVG is best - it stays sharp on a projector at any size. Otherwise PNG with a
transparent background, at least 400px on the long edge.

Avoid a logo baked onto a white or coloured tile; the page draws its own tile
and a second one inside it looks like a mistake.
